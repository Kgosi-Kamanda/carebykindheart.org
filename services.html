In-home care services

Two service lines. Our full attention.

We focus on senior care and disability support for adults in Spokane County. Both share the same intake process, the same hiring standards, and the same commitment to keeping the same caregiver in your home.

Senior care

In-home care for older adults who want to stay home

The goal isn't just safety. It's a life that still looks like theirs.

We help seniors with the practical things: bathing, dressing, medication reminders, meals, light housekeeping, transportation to appointments. We also provide companionship and engagement for clients who live alone. Because isolation is a health condition too, even if it doesn't appear on a discharge summary.

  • Personal care: bathing, grooming, dressing, mobility assistance
  • Medication reminders and management support
  • Meal preparation, nutrition monitoring, and grocery runs
  • Transportation and errand assistance
  • Companionship and social engagement
  • Overnight and live-in care where needed
  • Coordination with physicians and specialist teams

Independence, on your terms

Support looks different for everyone

Our job isn't to do everything for you. It's to help with exactly what you need, and nothing you don't.

Disability support

In-home support for adults with disabilities

Independence looks different for every person. Our job is to support the version of independence that is realistic and meaningful for each individual client, not the version that's easiest to staff.

We work with adults with physical, developmental, and cognitive disabilities. Our caregivers are matched based on the specific needs of each person, not assigned from whoever is next on a rotation.

  • Personal care and daily living assistance
  • Community access and transportation support
  • Skill-building activities and developmental support
  • Respite care for family caregivers, including overnight and weekend coverage
  • Coordination with specialists, therapists, and case managers

How payment works

We're upfront about where we are on pricing

We're a new company and we're still finalizing our formal payment options, including insurance billing. That means we won't post a rate that isn't real. What we will do is have an honest conversation about your situation and what's possible.

Private pay, available now

We accept private pay today. Contact us for a personalized quote based on your specific care needs and schedule.

Insurance & Medicaid, in progress

We're working on becoming an approved Medicaid provider in Washington. If insurance coverage matters to your decision, tell us and we'll give you a straight answer on where that process stands.
