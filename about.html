Our story

A local company, built by people who live here

Care by KindHeart was founded in 2026 by Stephen Guto and Felister Ndugire to bring dependable, attentive home care to families in Spokane County, Washington.

Small on purpose, for now

Care by KindHeart is a locally owned home care company based at 821 N Stanley St in Medical Lake, serving families throughout Spokane County. We started in 2026 with a simple focus: senior and elder care, and in-home support for adults with disabilities.

We're not trying to look bigger than we are. Our team is small right now, every caregiver is hired directly as an employee, and every one of them completes a state background check and 24 hours of paid orientation before their first visit. We'd rather grow carefully than grow fast.

As we build out our team and our processes, including bringing on a registered nurse for clinical assessments, we plan to expand what we offer. For now, we focus on doing right by the families we already serve.

At a glance

What guides us

Four principles. Not a poster. How we actually make decisions.

Evidence before adjectives

We don't tell families we're compassionate. We show them the in-home assessment, the background check we run on every caregiver, and the orientation every caregiver completes before their first visit. Claims are cheap. Specifics aren't.

Same caregiver, every time

Caregiver consistency is what families want and what the industry most consistently fails to deliver. We schedule specifically to keep primary caregiver relationships intact. It costs more to run that way. We do it anyway.

We tell you the cost upfront

We're a new company still finalizing our pricing structure, so we won't post a number that isn't real. What we will do is walk through your options honestly before you commit to anything.

The founders are personally involved

With a small team, decisions about your care plan don't get lost in layers of management. Stephen and Felister are directly reachable, and directly accountable.
