Cookie Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026. This policy explains the cookies used on carebykindheart.org and how you can control them.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help a site remember your preferences, understand how it's used, and, in some cases, support advertising. We also use similar technologies, such as local storage, for the same purposes.

CategoryPurposeCan you decline?
Essential Required for the site to function: remembering your cookie preference, your dark or light mode choice, and basic security protections. No — these are necessary for the site to work
Analytics Help us understand which pages are useful, how visitors move through the site, and where to improve. Data is aggregated and not used to identify you individually. Yes — declined by default until you accept
Functional Remember choices you make, such as a saved zip code, so you don't have to re-enter them. Yes

When you first visit our site, a banner lets you accept all cookies or limit us to essential cookies only. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for carebykindheart.org and reloading the page, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings directly. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their privacy or security settings menu.

Declining analytics cookies does not affect your ability to use the site, request a care assessment, or apply for a caregiving position.

We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies on this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated and, where required, we will request renewed consent.

Contact privacy@carebykindheart.org with questions about this Cookie Policy or our broader Privacy Policy.
