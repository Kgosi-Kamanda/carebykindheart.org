What the work actually looks like

Warm, unhurried time with real people

Not a checklist rushed through. A relationship, built one visit at a time.

Schedules published two weeks out

You know where you're working before the week starts. We schedule two weeks in advance and minimize last-minute changes. Your time outside work is yours.

A primary client relationship

You're matched with a primary client based on skills, personality, and availability. You're not scattered across a different house every day. Consistency is better for clients. It's also better for you.

Paid training, not onboarding paperwork

Every new caregiver goes through 24 hours of paid orientation before their first client visit, covering documentation, safety, and the specifics of who they'll be working with.

A direct employee, not a contractor

Every caregiver we hire is a W-2 employee, not a 1099 contractor. We'll share pay details directly with candidates once we open positions.
