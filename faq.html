Home care FAQ

Questions we get every week

These are the things families actually ask us, in the order they usually ask them. If you don't find what you need, call (908) 666-3853 and a real person will pick up.

Getting started

You talk directly to one of our founders, not a call center. The first call is about understanding your situation: who needs care, what kind, and what the timeline looks like. You won't get a quote in that call. You'll get clarity on next steps and a date for an in-home assessment.

We visit your home, review daily living needs, observe the home environment, and talk to both the client and the family. It produces a care plan that documents specific needs and a schedule recommendation. Nothing about caregiver matching happens until the assessment is complete. We're currently working on adding a registered nurse to lead clinical portions of this process.

Care can often start the same day you call. For urgent situations, our fastest realistic response time is 1 to 3 hours.

Real answers, no scripts

Straight talk, even when the answer is "not yet"

We'd rather tell you honestly where we stand than promise something we can't deliver.

Payment & insurance

We're a new company still finalizing our formal pricing structure, so we won't post a rate that isn't real. During your first call, we'll give you a straight, personalized answer based on your specific situation.

Not yet. We currently accept private pay, and we're working toward becoming an approved Medicaid provider in Washington. If insurance coverage matters to your decision, tell us during your first call and we'll be upfront about where that process stands.

Caregivers & safety

Yes, in the typical situation. We assign a primary caregiver to each client based on skill match, scheduling compatibility, and personality fit. That person is your regular caregiver. If they're unavailable, a familiar backup from our team covers. We're explicit about this in our scheduling policies because consistency is the single thing families most often tell us they couldn't find elsewhere.

A state-required background check on every caregiver, every hire, plus 24 hours of paid orientation before their first client visit. We don't waive either step regardless of how urgent a staffing need is.

Our regular hours are Monday through Friday, 8am to 6pm, and Saturday 8am to 3pm. For urgent situations, we can often respond within 1 to 3 hours. Call (908) 666-3853 and we'll do our best to help.

Coverage

We're happy to talk through dementia and memory-care needs directly during your first call. As a small team, we can be honest upfront about whether a specific situation is a good match before we start.

We currently serve Spokane County, Washington. We don't have a timeline for expanding beyond that yet, but ask us directly if you're just outside the area.
