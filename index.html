What we do

Care built around the person, not the condition

We're a young company focused on doing two things well: helping older adults stay safely at home, and supporting adults with disabilities who want more independence. As we grow, we plan to carefully add more ways to help.

Senior & elder care

Help with daily living, personal care, medication reminders, and companionship for older adults who want to stay home. Same caregiver, every visit.

Disability support (adults)

How it works

From your first call to your first visit

01

One call

Tell us about your situation. No pressure. Our intake coordinators are trained to listen before they recommend anything.

02

In-home assessment

We visit your home, review daily living needs, and build a written care plan tailored to the person, not a template.

03

Caregiver match

We match you with a primary caregiver whose schedule, skills, and personality fit your household. You keep the same person. That's not an accident.

04

Ongoing coordination

Your care coordinator checks in regularly. If something changes, the plan changes. You always have a direct line to our team.
