Senior care

In-home care for older adults who want to stay home

The goal isn't just safety. It's a life that still looks like theirs. We help seniors stay in the home they know, with a caregiver who actually knows them.

Ask about senior care See what's included

Isolation is a health condition too

We help seniors with the practical things: bathing, dressing, medication reminders, meals, light housekeeping, transportation to appointments. But a lot of what we do isn't on any checklist. It's noticing that a client hasn't left the house in a week, or that they've stopped finishing meals, and saying something before it becomes a crisis.

Even if it doesn't appear on a discharge summary, loneliness affects health outcomes as much as any diagnosis. That's why companionship and social engagement are built into every senior care plan, not billed as an extra.

We assess every client before care starts: daily routines, home environment, mobility, and family input. The care plan that comes out of that visit is specific to the person, not a template.

What's included

Every senior care plan can include

Personal care

Bathing, grooming, dressing, and mobility assistance, handled with patience and privacy.

Medication support

Reminders and management so doses aren't missed, doubled, or taken at the wrong time.

Meals & errands

Meal preparation, nutrition monitoring, grocery runs, and transportation to appointments.

Companionship

Social engagement and conversation for clients living alone, not just supervision.

Overnight & live-in care

Extended coverage when a family needs more than a few daytime hours a week.

Physician coordination

We communicate directly with your physicians and specialist teams, not just with you.
