Disability support

In-home support for adults with disabilities

Independence looks different for every person. Our job is to support the version of independence that's realistic and meaningful for each individual client, not the version that's easiest to staff.

Ask about disability support See what's included

We don't put a general aide in a skilled situation

We work with adults with physical, developmental, and cognitive disabilities. Our caregivers are matched based on the specific needs of each person, not assigned from whoever is next on a rotation.

That matching process starts with an in-home assessment: mobility, communication needs, behavioral considerations, existing therapies, and what the person and their family define as meaningful independence. The care plan that comes out of it is built around that answer, not around a standard service menu.

We also work directly with the specialists, therapists, and case managers already involved in a client's care, so our caregivers reinforce what's already working instead of introducing a separate, disconnected routine.

What's included

Every disability support plan can include

Personal care

Daily living assistance built around each person's specific abilities and needs.

Community access

Transportation and support for getting out into school, work, and community life.

Skill-building

Developmental support and structured activities tied to each person's goals.

Family respite

Overnight and weekend coverage so family caregivers get real time off.

Specialist coordination

Direct communication with therapists and case managers already on the team.

Honest payment conversations

We're working toward becoming a Medicaid provider. Until then, we'll tell you exactly where that process stands.
